NEUROBRIDGE
Making workplaces work for every mind.
Core idea:
A digital workplace platform that helps neurodivergent employees understand and communicate their working preferences, while giving managers and HR practical tools to create a more inclusive workplace.

The workplace wasn't designed for everyone.

•	Communicate in the same way 
•	Perform well in traditional interviews 
•	Attend frequent meetings 
•	Work in noisy environments 
•	Understand vague instructions 
•	Adapt to rigid processes

STEP 4 — AI WORKPLACE ASSISTANT
This could be one of the strongest features.
Call it something like:
🤖 NeuroBridge Assistant
The employee can type:
“I have a presentation tomorrow and don't know where to start.”
The assistant could respond with:
Let's break it down.
1. Define your topic — 15 min
2. Create 3–5 key points — 20 min
3. Build your slides — 45 min
4. Practice — 20 min
5. Final review — 10 min
________________________________________
Another example:
Employee:
“My manager gave me a task but I don't understand what they want.”
The assistant could help turn the instruction into:
What is the task?
What is the deadline?
What does success look like?
What information do you need?
It could also help draft:
“Hi, just to make sure I understand the task correctly, my understanding is…”
This makes the AI useful without pretending to diagnose or treat anyone.

7. MANAGER — "WHAT SHOULD I DO?"
This could be a very useful feature.
Manager enters:
“My employee isn't participating in meetings.”
NeuroBridge could provide possible workplace approaches:
Try:
1.	Send questions before the meeting. 
2.	Give the employee time to prepare. 
3.	Don't assume silence means lack of engagement. 
4.	Offer alternative ways to contribute. 
5.	Follow up after the meeting. 
Then:
Avoid assuming behaviour = lack of ability or motivation.
This is where your platform becomes an education + action tool.
